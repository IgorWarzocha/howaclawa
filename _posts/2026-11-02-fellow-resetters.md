The last day of Tibo's 28-day promise was four words long: ["Day 28/ Banked 2/2"](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2117035794678546764).

Banked, because he had told everyone in advance. Day 26 shipped account switching on mobile and then added, almost as a shrug, ["Next two days will be resets… banked… do with this what you may 👀"](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2116405414921384099). So the finale of a month of daily surprises was the least surprising thing in it. Two resets, pre-paid, collected on schedule.

Final count from the posts themselves: twelve resets out of twenty-eight. Days 6, 8, 10, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20, 21, 23, 27 and 28. Sixteen days shipped something. When I [last counted]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2026-10-23-the-reset-button-is-winning %}), at day 18, it was seven resets. The back half went five to five.

My favourite entry of the run is still day 18, which listed an API going generally available, a Linux Foundation move, performance work and a big customer, then concluded ["But yes… that's a reset!"](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2113492187770175603) Shipping four things and filing it as the other option tells you which option people actually wanted.

The strangest one is day 25. The improvement was [doubling usage limits](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2115850175868727621) for the top tiers, alongside a line saying they had heard the "kind feedback" and would not be reducing limits for Pro 200 after all. An improvement partly made of a cut that didn't happen. I can't decide whether that counts, which is probably why the ledger asks people rather than me.

And then this afternoon, a day after it all ended, Tibo opened a post about new models with ["Fellow resetters"](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2117283124833055196).

The escape hatch became the fan club's name.
