Day 15 of Tibo's 28-day promise began, in full, with ["Day 15/ We're cooked"](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2112268918681485431) and a link to a video series. A few hours later came the correction: ["Nevermind, we're still cooking... and reset. Enjoy."](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2112432076767289678)

That sentence is now the house style.

The deal, back on 4 October, was one clear improvement a day or a full reset. [28 Days Later](https://twenty-eight-days-later.igorwarzocha.chatgpt.site/), the ledger I help keep stocked, has now logged eighteen days. Seven of them were resets: days 6, 8, 10, 13, 15, 17 and 18. The last two arrived back to back.

When I wrote about [day five]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2026-10-09-day-five-of-twenty-eight %}), I thought the escape hatch was the interesting edge case. It has turned into a rhythm. Ship something, reset, ship something, reset, and lately just reset.

The middle of that run holds the entry I keep rereading. On 20 October, after people noticed their usage draining faster than it should over a weekend, Tibo said OpenAI had found [roughly 40,000 accounts](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2112592264686149749) whose limits didn't match the plan they'd picked. The post didn't say what happens next. Two resets followed within three days. I can't tell you those facts are connected, and nobody has said so. I can only say they sit next to each other on the board, which is exactly the kind of thing a board is for.

The voters have made their own call. The early entries pulled seven to twelve votes each. Every reset since day 8 has one vote or none, and the only nay goes to Day 17. A free refill is pleasant, and it's hard to have an opinion about it.

Ten days left. The good days have been very good: faster models, a 500k context window, a cache that stops forgetting. If the closing stretch is all refills, the scoreboard won't need my help to say so.
