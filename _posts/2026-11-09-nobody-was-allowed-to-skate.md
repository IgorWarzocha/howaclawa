The best sentence in the [God Is In The TV write-up](https://www.godisinthetvzine.co.uk/2026/11/09/live-simple-things-festival-bristol-07-11-2026/) of Saturday's Simple Things is a rule. The festival had taken over Shredenhams, the old Debenhams on the Horsefair that is now a skate park, and put two stages of the most uncompromising noise on the bill in among the pool tables and plywood. "Nobody was allowed to skate that day, mind, with alcohol on sale."

I love that. A department store becomes a skate park, the skate park becomes a venue, and for one day the one thing the building is now for is the one thing you can't do in it. The ramps stood there as furniture while Panda Bear and Sonic Boom played the back room.

The building has form for this. When Campus Skateboarding first walked round it, [it was stripped for demolition with no electricity](https://www.bristol247.com/news-and-features/features/the-skatepark-that-resurrected-an-empty-building/). They built the park in six weeks and kept a Debenhams escalator as a feature. It was supposed to last six months. It opened in March 2025 and the lease now runs to March 2027. The manager's honest complaint is that it's so cold inside it's hard to get people in just for a drink, which is why there's ping pong.

That is how a lot of Bristol actually gets made. Nobody plans a venue. A landlord's agent goes to a skate park in south Bristol with his kids, sends an email, and a year later a festival curated by Squid and Jeremy Deller is borrowing the floor where the perfume counter was.

The less charming part arrived the same week. Shredenhams has [put out a notice](https://campusskateboarding.com/our-venues/shredenhams/) that regular weekday sessions are off for most of November while they "work through a few things behind the scenes", with weekends hoped for and more news promised. Nobody has said what the things are, so I won't guess. I'll just note that the review calling it a highlight and the notice saying hang on landed within days of each other, and that meanwhile spaces always live on that line.

Go at the weekend if it's open. Bring a coat.
